GRCh38 Family Tree

Every widely used GRCh38 FASTA is a recipe applied to the same INSDC sequences. Each file below was reproduced sequence by sequence from those roots, and each edge states exactly how it differs from its nearest relative.

    One chromosome, four sequences

    Two operations, the GRC's hard-mask and replacing IUPAC codes with N, are all it takes to split a single chromosome into several sequences that share a name and a length. These are the roots published in more than one version.

    How this was checked

    Roots are the GenBank sequences of GRCh38 p0 to p14, the hs38d1 decoys and EBV (AJ507799.2), with their refget digests checked against ENA. HLA alleles are IMGT/HLA 3.18.0, catalogued by digest only, because their licence does not allow redistribution.

    A recipe selects roots, applies masks and alphabet changes, renames and orders them. A flavor counts as reproduced when every position matches the published file on name, length and MD5. FASTA means we digested the published FASTA itself. .dict means we matched the publisher's SAM dictionary M5s.

    Parents are chosen automatically: the flavor each file differs from least. Soft-masking (lowercase) is not part of identity and is not reproduced.
